Stage 1 - one-time setup (see ../README.md and ../docs/BUILDING.md)
====================================================================
In the Ubuntu (WSL) window, with your own retail ISO:

    bash ~/black-mesa-tennis/stage1/tt_android_stage1.sh "/mnt/c/path/to/TableTennis.iso"

Takes 1-2 hours. Re-running is safe; finished steps are skipped.
If it fails, attach ~/tt-android/tt-stage1-logs.zip to an issue (logs only, no game files).
